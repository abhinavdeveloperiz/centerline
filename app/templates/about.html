About Us

Who We Are

Healthcare Infrastructure Solution Provider

Founded in 2024, Centerline Technical Services was established to address the critical infrastructure needs of the healthcare industry. We specialize in healthcare ventilation systems, modular construction, electrical infrastructure, medical gas systems and infection control solutions.

Our goal is to enhance healthcare facility efficiency, safety and adaptability through innovative engineering solutions that support modern medical environments.

Message From Management

Building Safer Healthcare Environments

At Centerline, we are committed to transforming healthcare infrastructure to meet the evolving needs of modern medical care. With a clear mission to enhance efficiency, safety and adaptability, we deliver innovative solutions in healthcare ventilation, modular construction, electrical practices and infection control systems.

We understand the challenges healthcare providers face, from maintaining optimal air quality and reliable power systems to ensuring compliance with strict regulations. Our team continuously develops advanced technologies and tailored solutions that prioritize patient care and operational excellence.
